Endless Space

Privacy policy · last updated 8 October 2026

Endless Space is a game made by Tom Black. This page says what the game keeps about you, why, and how to have it deleted. Short version: no names, emails or contacts are needed to play (signing in with Google, to keep your account, is your choice), nothing is sold, and you can delete everything from inside the game.

What's kept, and why

  • An anonymous account. The game signs you in without asking for anything: a random id, so your progress can be saved and your scores put on the leaderboards.
  • Your Google account, if you sign in with it. In the Android app you can choose to keep your account with Google, so you can get it back on another phone. Then your Google account's id and email address are kept with your game account, only to sign you back in. Nothing is posted to Google and nothing else is read from it.
  • Your progress. Your settings, looks, credits, cores, goals and stats, on your device and as a cloud save tied to the anonymous account.
  • Your runs. The score, distance and time of runs sent to the leaderboards, with the steering of ranked runs, to check they're genuine.
  • Your pilot name. The name you pick (or the one made up for you) shows on the leaderboards next to your scores.
  • What other players see. On the leaderboards, other players see your pilot name, your scores, the looks on your ship, whether you have premium, and, when they tap you, your service record: a summary of your stats (rank, XP, runs, time played, distance, furthest level, best chain, near misses and pickups), your best score on each board, and the month you started. Your account id, Google account and email are never shown.
  • Purchases. If you buy something, Apple or Google handle the payment. The game sees what was bought, not your payment details.

Who else is involved

  • Supabase stores the account, cloud save, runs and leaderboards.
  • Google confirms who you are when you sign in with Google (only if you choose to).
  • RevenueCat checks purchases with Apple and Google, using the anonymous account id.
  • Google AdMob shows the optional ads you choose to watch for a reward (on the phone apps only). AdMob may use your device's advertising id to show and measure ads. You're asked for consent where the law needs it (and on iPhone, whether apps may track you); you can change your answer in the game's settings or your phone's settings. Premium removes ads.

There are no analytics or tracking beyond this, and nothing is sold.

How long it's kept

For as long as you play. Delete your account and it's gone: your account, cloud save, runs, scores and name are deleted at once. Records of purchases are kept, without the account, as stores and tax rules need.

Deleting your data

In the game: settings, then "delete my account and data". It deletes everything on the server and on your device. Purchases can still be restored from the App Store or Google Play afterwards. If you can't open the game, email the address below and it will be done for you.

Children

The game isn't made for children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from them. If you think a child has used it, get in touch and their data will be deleted.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask what's kept about you, have it corrected or deleted, or take a copy. Email the address below.
